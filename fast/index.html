About these figures
  • What they are. The average yearly use of NHS services, and its cost, for people in England with the characteristics entered: an average over everyone like this, including people who use no care that year. They are not a prediction for one person and should not be used for decisions about anyone's care.
  • Services. Primary care contacts, prescription items, outpatient attendances, hospital admissions (inpatient spells) and A&E attendances. Other services, such as community and social care, are not included.
  • Costs. NHS costs at 2019/20 prices: primary care contacts at PSSRU-based costs per contact; prescriptions at the Prescription Cost Analysis net ingredient cost per item; hospital activity at the national schedules of NHS costs.
  • Data. CPRD Aurum primary care records of people aged 20 to 100, linked to Hospital Episode Statistics, 2013 to 2019; practice patterns of 2019 (calendar year) or 2019/20 (financial year).
  • Details not known. If exactly the known details match one of the models fitted without the others, that model is used. Otherwise the unknown details are averaged over people like this, filled in from how characteristics and conditions occur together. An unknown survival is averaged over the risk of death.
  • Calculation. The averages are computed without simulation (the expected use is exact; the expected cost is computed to the precision shown). The spread comes from simulated people. Results are reproducible: the same profile always gives the same figures.
  • Privacy. R runs in your browser and nothing you enter is sent anywhere. A copied link contains the profiles entered.

Proof of concept. Predictions of the hcucost models, computed in your browser by the package's C++ core compiled to WebAssembly (no R needed).
